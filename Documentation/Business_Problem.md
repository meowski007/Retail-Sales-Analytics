# Executive360 – Retail Intelligence & Strategic Decision Platform

## Problem Statement

A global retail company has experienced strong sales growth across multiple regions but inconsistent profitability.

Senior leadership requires a centralized analytics platform to identify:

- Which markets deserve further investment
- Which products generate revenue but reduce profitability
- How discounts impact margins
- Which customer segments drive business value
- Opportunities to improve operational efficiency

The objective is to convert transactional data into executive-level insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

## Primary Stakeholders

- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Head of Sales
- Regional Managers
- Product Strategy Team

---

## Success Criteria

- Increase profitability
- Improve pricing decisions
- Optimize product portfolio
- Identify growth opportunities
- Support executive decision-making
